Privacy Policy — Retro Arcade Games

Effective date: November 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application “Retro Arcade Games” (“the App”) handles information. The App is published by the developer (“we”, “us”). This policy applies to all users of the App.

Summary

  • We do not collect, store, or share personal data.
  • We do not use third‑party analytics, ads SDKs, or trackers.
  • The App works fully offline and does not require an account.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No names, emails, phone numbers, or other identifiers
  • No precise location data
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or usage profiles

Device Permissions

The App does not request sensitive device permissions. It renders graphics, audio, and haptics locally within the app sandbox.

Children’s Privacy

The App may be used by people of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Third‑Party Services

The App does not integrate third‑party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs. Audio/visual assets are bundled locally. The App is built with Flutter, which runs locally on device.

Data Security

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no server‑side storage. Any game state (e.g., scores) is retained only on the device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted at the same public URL where this policy is hosted. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
